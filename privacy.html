Privacy policy

Effective 7 October 2026

financeguru is a YouTube channel run by an individual based in Hungary. To publish its videos, and short clips of them on Instagram and TikTok, the channel uses a private publishing tool. This policy explains what that tool and this website do with data.

Who uses the publishing tool

Only the owner of the financeguru channel. The tool is not offered to the public, and it never accesses anyone else's Google, Instagram or TikTok account.

Google and YouTube data the tool accesses

The tool uses YouTube API Services, with the channel owner's permission, to:

  • upload videos, thumbnails and their descriptions to the financeguru channel, and set them to public or scheduled;
  • read public information about videos in the channel's topic area, to see what viewers are interested in;
  • read view counts and analytics for the channel's own videos, to measure how they perform.

Instagram data the tool accesses

With the channel owner's permission, through Instagram's API, the tool posts the channel's own short videos as Reels to the owner's Instagram professional account, and reads that account's ID and username to do so. It does not read messages, followers or anyone else's content.

TikTok data the tool accesses

With the channel owner's permission, through TikTok's Login Kit and Content Posting API, the tool:

  • reads the owner's basic TikTok profile information (account ID, display name) to confirm which account is connected;
  • uploads the channel's own short videos to the owner's TikTok drafts (scope video.upload), where the owner reviews and posts them in the TikTok app.

It does not post on its own, and it does not read videos, comments, followers or messages.

How the data is used and stored

  • The data is used only to publish the channel's videos and plan future ones. It is never sold, never used for advertising, and never combined with data about other people.
  • The sign-in tokens (Google, Instagram, TikTok) and the performance numbers are stored on a private server controlled by the channel owner, and deleted when the tool is disconnected.
  • Titles and performance numbers of the channel's own videos may be processed by an AI service (Anthropic's Claude) to suggest topics for future videos. No other Google, Instagram or TikTok user data is shared with anyone.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time: for Google on the Google security settings page, for Instagram in Settings > Website permissions > Apps and websites, and for TikTok in Settings and privacy > Security > Apps and services. Stored data is then deleted within 30 days.

Platform terms

By using a tool built on YouTube API Services, the channel owner agrees to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. Instagram and TikTok handle data as described in the Instagram Privacy Policy and the TikTok Privacy Policy.

Visitors to this website

This website does not use cookies, analytics or tracking. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may process technical data such as your IP address to deliver the page, as described in GitHub's privacy statement. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts.
